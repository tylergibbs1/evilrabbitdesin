# Sources

## Who he is

**Evil Rabbit** is Nicolás Garro Zambrano — founding (now principal) brand designer at Vercel, working alongside CEO Guillermo Rauch since ~2016 (joined when the company was ZEIT). He works across brand, product, and type. He is **not** the same person as Guillermo Rauch — a conflation that appears in some search summaries; disregard it.

- Portfolio: https://evilrabb.it (and https://www.evilrabbit.com) — deliberately near-empty; minimalism as a signal
- Bio: https://vercel.com/about/evilrabbit
- GitHub: https://github.com/evilrabbit — portfolio repos (`www`, `main`, `website`) show his actual CSS choices
- X/Twitter: https://x.com/evilrabbit_

## Sourcing caveats — read before quoting

- **He gives almost no interviews.** There is no long-form talk or podcast where he lays out a philosophy. If you need "his voice," the Geist Pixel essay is essentially the only deep well. Don't invent quotes.
- **Do not attribute the Geist system's institutional language to him personally.** "Simplicity, minimalism, speed," the Swiss-design framing, and the product-design operating contract reflect his direction but are Vercel/institutional copy, not his stated words.
- **Geist is a directed team effort**, not a solo draw — designers Andrés Briganti and Mateo Zaragoza and font developer Guido Ferreyra (via basement.studio) did the typeface work. Credit accordingly.
- **Claims I could not substantiate from a primary source** (so don't put them in his mouth): that he personally champions "1px precision," "less but better," or black-and-white "as ideology" in those words. The work is consistent with all of it; citable first-person statements are not.
- **The "1440 UPM" font grid figure is unverified** — see the note in `geist-system.md`.
- **Two Vercel Blues:** legacy `#0070f3` vs. current Geist `blue-700` `#006bff`. Both are real; use `#006bff` for current-system work.
- Some visual signatures in `visual-language.md` are observed directly from his own portfolio CSS (`#eaeaea` borders, `#fafafa` fills, `#0076ff`/`#0070f3` links, `#999` muted text, backdrop blur, ~980px max-width) — these are reverse-engineered from his code, accurate as *his choices*, not official system tokens.
